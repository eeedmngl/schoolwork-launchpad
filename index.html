HOW THIS DESK WORKS

The 5 Ground Rules

Skipping a day isn't failure—just pick up the next tiny step.
RULE 01

Cap Today at 3

1 Must-Do, 1 Should-Do, 1 Quick Win. A 14-item to-do list just makes your brain hit the brakes.

RULE 02

Shrink Step One

Make the first step embarrassingly small—like "Open the doc and type my name."

RULE 03

10-Minute Deal

You only owe the assignment 10 minutes. When the chime rings, you have full permission to stop.

RULE 04

Pre-Pick Your Exit

Decide before you start what you'll do if you hit a wall (text a friend, do 1 example, or email teacher).

RULE 05

Respect Hard Stops

Sleep protects tomorrow's focus. When your hard stop time hits, pack up guilt-free.
